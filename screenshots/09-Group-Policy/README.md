# Group Policy Screenshots

This folder contains screenshots documenting the Group Policy configuration implemented in the NOVATECH Enterprise Infrastructure lab.

## Screenshots

### 01 - Group Policy Management
Shows the Group Policy Management Console (GPMC) with the Active Directory domain and Organizational Unit hierarchy.

### 02 - Group Policy Objects
Displays all custom Group Policy Objects created for workstation security and department drive mappings.

### 03 - Finance Drive Mapping Policy
Shows the Finance Drive Mapping Group Policy Preference that automatically maps the Finance network share to users.

## Purpose

These screenshots provide visual evidence of:

- Centralized Group Policy management
- Custom GPO deployment
- Drive Mapping using Group Policy Preferences
- Enterprise administration best practices
